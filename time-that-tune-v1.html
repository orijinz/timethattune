How to Play

  1. You'll see an artist and a decade. Set the slider to the number of seconds you want to hear — your goal is to choose the fewest seconds you'll need to identify the song that's about to play.
  2. Hit Play. The clip plays for exactly the time you choose, then stops. The song clip is not generally the beginning of the song, it is a preview of some part of the song.
  3. Know it already? Hit Guess now — I know it! to skip the rest of the clip. Your score becomes the average of your bet and how long you actually listened — so it's always worth stopping early once you're sure, but betting low and being right is still your best score.
  4. Six song titles appear — one correct, five wrong. Pick the correct one.
  5. Wrong picks get eliminated but cost you 15 seconds each. Keep guessing until you get the right answer.

Your score is seconds listened (or, if you solved it early, the average of your bet and actual time) + seconds spent answering + 15s per wrong guess. Lower is better.
