Time That Tune (TTT) is a strategic music‑guessing game built around one challenge:

How quickly can you identify a song?

1. Choose Your Music Set
  • Song Decade — you see an artist; choose how many seconds you think you'll need to identify one of their songs.
  • Showtunes — you see a musical; bet on how many seconds of a song from that show you'll need.
  • Themes — you see a TV or film category (e.g. 1970s Drama Film) and guess the theme song.

Use the slider to set your bet. Lower is riskier — and more rewarding if you're right.

2. Listen to the Clip

Hit ▶ Play. The game plays exactly the seconds you chose — then stops.

The clip is not the start of the song. It's a preview from somewhere inside the track, so you can't rely on intros or famous openings.

3. Make Your Guess

Six titles appear — one correct, five wrong:

Bohemian Rhapsody
Hotel California
Don't Stop Me Now
Go Your Own Way
Roxanne ✓
Africa

Wrong answers flash red and disappear. Each wrong guess costs +20 seconds. Keep guessing until you find the right one.

4. "Guess Now — I Know It!"

If you recognize the song before your chosen time runs out, hit:

Guess now — I know it!

This stops the clip early so you can answer. You do get assessed a small "skip penalty:"

Skip penalty = average of your bet and the time you actually listened

So stopping early is always worth it once you're sure — but betting low and being right is how you chase elite scores.

5. Scoring — Lower is Better

Your final score is:

 Seconds listened
+Skip penalty (if you stopped early)
+Seconds spent answering
+20 seconds per wrong guess

Bet smart, listen carefully, guess quickly, and have fun!
